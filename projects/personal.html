Frame
React · Progressive Web App

An offline-first study app for drilling Mandarin grammar patterns, built because I'm living the problem it solves while studying for my move to Tsinghua. It installs straight to your phone as a PWA, no app store, works with no connection at all, and grades your answers with AI whenever you're online.

Offline-first by design

The whole point is that it works without signal. When there's no connection, grading falls back to a local grader instead of just failing, with a visible offline indicator so you always know which mode you're in.

Speaking practice

A voice chatbot you can actually talk to, which listens to your spoken Mandarin and corrects it in real time.

Built for retention, not just review

A mistake-repeater mode resurfaces the grammar patterns you actually get wrong instead of drilling everything equally, alongside a streak system and a full set of grammar tests.
